Anamor's Block Puzzle

A sweet, relaxing block puzzle

Coming soon to Google Play

Drag glossy candy blocks onto an 8×8 board, fill rows and columns to clear them, and chain clears into combos. No timers in Classic mode, no falling pieces – just a relaxing puzzle you can play offline.

  • Classic endless mode in three difficulties, a 3-minute Timed mode and a Daily Puzzle
  • Anamor's Quest: 100 levels in five seasonal chapters with unlockable block styles
  • Plays fully offline – no account, no sign-in
  • Ads only if you choose to watch one for a second chance – never forced
