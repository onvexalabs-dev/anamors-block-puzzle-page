Privacy Policy

Anamor's Block Puzzle (Android) · Effective: 13 October 2026

Short version: the game has no user accounts, no own servers and no analytics. Your progress stays on your device. The only third-party data processing happens through Google: optional rewarded video ads (Google AdMob) and the Google Play store itself. In the EEA, UK and Switzerland you are asked for consent before ads are personalised, and you can change your choice in the game at any time.

1. Controller

Manfred Uschan (private individual developer, label “Onvexa Labs”)

No data protection officer is appointed (not legally required).

2. Data stored only on your device

The game saves your current game, high scores, Daily Puzzle results, Quest stars, the selected block style, settings (sound, music, vibration, animations), whether the tutorial was completed and when you last chose to watch an ad (to limit how often ads are offered) in files inside the app's private storage. This data is never transmitted to us. You can erase it at any time by clearing the app's storage in Android settings or by uninstalling the app.

3. Optional rewarded ads – Google AdMob

The app contains the Google Mobile Ads SDK, provided by Google Ireland Limited, Gordon House, Barrow Street, Dublin 4, Ireland (“Google”). A video ad is shown only when you actively choose to watch one (for a second chance after a game ends) and confirm it in a dialog that always offers “No thanks”. There are no banners, interstitials or app-open ads, and nothing in the game requires watching an ad.

When the SDK is active, Google automatically processes:

  • IP address (from which an approximate location may be derived)
  • Device and advertising identifiers (Android advertising ID, app set ID)
  • Information about app and ad interactions (e.g. app launch, video views, taps)
  • Diagnostic and performance information of the SDK
  • Device information (model, operating system version, language, network type)

Purposes: delivering and measuring ads, frequency capping, fraud prevention and security, and – only with your consent – personalised advertising.

Consent (EEA, UK, Switzerland): before the ad SDK is started, a consent form from Google's User Messaging Platform (a certified consent management platform under the IAB Transparency & Consent Framework) asks for your choices. Your decision is stored on your device. You can change it at any time via Settings ▸ Ad privacy choices. If you do not consent, Google may still show non-personalised or limited ads.

Legal basis: your consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR; § 165(3) Austrian Telecommunications Act 2021) and, for non-personalised ads, fraud prevention and security, our legitimate interest in financing a free game (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).

Transfers outside the EU: Google may process data in the USA. Google LLC is certified under the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework; additionally, Standard Contractual Clauses apply.

You can reset or delete your advertising ID in Android settings. More information: How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services · Google Privacy Policy.

4. Google Play distribution

Downloading the game from Google Play requires a Google account; Google processes the related data under its own responsibility. We receive only aggregated, non-identifying statistics from the Google Play Console (e.g. installs, crashes reported by Android, ratings).

5. Contact by email

If you email us, we process your email address and message to answer you (Art. 6(1)(b) or (f) GDPR). We delete the correspondence when your request is resolved, unless statutory retention obligations apply.

6. This website

This website is hosted on GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc., 88 Colin P. Kelly Jr. St., San Francisco, CA 94107, USA). When you visit it, GitHub processes your IP address and technical request data to deliver the pages and ensure security (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). See the GitHub Privacy Statement. The website sets no cookies; a language preference may be stored in your browser's local storage only when you press EN/DE.

7. What we do not do

  • No user accounts, no sign-in, no own servers, no analytics or tracking SDKs by us
  • No in-app purchases
  • No access to contacts, photos, files, microphone, camera or precise location
  • No sale of personal data by us

8. Children

The game is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children. Ads are requested with a maximum content rating suitable for general audiences (PG).

9. Retention

On-device data stays until you clear it or uninstall. Data processed by Google is retained according to Google's policies. Emails: see section 5.

10. Your rights

You have the right to access, rectification, erasure, restriction, data portability, objection and withdrawal of consent. Because we do not hold your game data, most requests are fulfilled on your device (see Data deletion). You may lodge a complaint with the Austrian Data Protection Authority (Datenschutzbehörde, Barichgasse 40–42, 1030 Vienna, dsb.gv.at).

11. Changes

We will update this policy if the app changes (e.g. new ad formats). The effective date above shows the current version.
